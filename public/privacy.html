Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

King of Diamonds is a game you play in your browser without creating an account. This page explains what information is used and why.

What stays on your device

Your player name, avatar, settings (language, sound, voice) and match record are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They are not sent to us except as described below. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

What the game server sees

When you play online (Quick match or rooms), your player name, avatar and the numbers you pick are sent to the game server so other players at your table can see them. The server keeps this only while the match is running. Like most web servers, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses for security and troubleshooting.

Voice chat

Voice chat is off until you turn on your microphone. Audio is sent directly between players' browsers (peer to peer) and is not recorded or stored by us. Your browser will ask for microphone permission first, and you can turn the mic off at any time.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Children

The game shows cartoon violence (players are eliminated by a turret) and is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact
