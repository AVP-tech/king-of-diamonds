Microphone not working?

Open the game in Chrome or Safari, not inside Instagram, WhatsApp or another in-app browser. Tap the mic button once and press Allow. Voice works best on Wi-Fi. On mobile data it can take a few seconds to connect. Check that the page is https and that your phone is not on silent for media.

Dropped out of a game?

If you switch apps or lock your phone, your seat is held for a few minutes and a bot plays it for you. Come back and you continue where you were. The game keeps your screen awake while you wait for a match.

Cannot join a friend?

Check the 5 letter room code. Rooms close when everyone leaves, and a match that has started cannot be joined. At public Quick Match tables there is one player per network.

Friends and chat

Share your friend code or invite link from the Friends screen. Both of you need to accept before you can see each other online, send invites and message. You can remove a friend at any time.

Play fair, be kind

Be respectful in chat and voice. Do not share personal details with people you do not know. Report abuse to us and include the room code.

Report a problem

Write what you were doing, the room code if you have one, your device and browser, and a screenshot if you can. We reply as soon as we can.
